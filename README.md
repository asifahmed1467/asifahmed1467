### About Me

- **Computer Science & Engineering** undergraduate specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- **R&D Intern at REVE Systems**, developing voice AI solutions, LLM-powered pipelines and data workflows
- Focused on **Bangla Speech & NLP**: text-to-speech, homograph disambiguation and speech representation models (wav2vec2)
- Experienced in **fine-tuning** transformer models and applying **model quantization** for efficient deployment
